First Time?

Your First Event?
We've Got You.

はじめてでも、大丈夫。

HARMONY is for friendship — not dating or pick-ups. No sales or recruiting, and no one gets judged on their language level.

Can I come alone?

Yes. We arrange the seating, so it's easy to join in even if you come on your own.

Do I need to speak English?

No. Language exchange is half Japanese, half English, and your level doesn't matter.

Do I need to speak Japanese?

No. Half of each language exchange is in English, and plenty of people speak slowly.

Is it OK for beginners?

Yes. Your language level doesn't matter, and the events are made for first-timers too.

How do I register?

Pick an event and tap “See Details & Join”. No Meetup account needed.

How do I pay?

Cash or PayPay on the day. We can't take credit cards.

What if I'm late?

No problem — we'll seat you at the next rotation.
